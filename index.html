UK Pension Rules & Modeling Assumptions

25% Tax-Free Lump Sum (PCLS)

Under UK rules, individuals can typically withdraw up to 25% of their pension pot tax-free (Pension Commencement Lump Sum). This is subject to the UK statutory Lump Sum Allowance limit of £268,275 (unless lifetime allowance protections apply). The remaining 75% is taxed as income under UK income tax bands upon drawdown.

UK Public State Pension & Triple Lock

The UK State Pension is protected by the Triple Lock mechanism (increasing by the highest of inflation, average wage growth, or 2.5%). Per the user model specification, it is projected to grow in line with the selected inflation rate, preserving 100% of its real purchasing power in today's money.

Nominal vs Real (Today's Money)

Future nominal values show the actual pounds and pence in the year of retirement. Real values discount these future sums by $(1 + \text{inflation})^N$ to show what that money can actually buy today, preventing the illusion of wealth created by decades of inflation compounding.

Auto-Enrolment & Tax Relief

UK workplace pensions mandate a minimum 8% total contribution (typically 5% employee and 3% employer). Contributions into registered pensions benefit from UK tax relief at source (or salary sacrifice National Insurance relief).
